Mr. P. Thirunavukkarasu
Founder & Director

Metallurgical engineer turned industrialist, with nearly three decades building businesses across engineering, manufacturing, and global trade. A graduate in Metallurgy from PSG College of Technology, Coimbatore, he began his career with Eicher in industrial engineering and manufacturing, then moved into textiles and apparel with Knit Fab. In 1998, he founded his own apparel export business, growing it into an international operation over the next 27 years — building deep experience in manufacturing, exports, and global markets. In August 2025, he stepped away to found Thiru Synergy, bringing that same operator's discipline to building a long-term renewable energy enterprise.

thiru@thirusynergy.com
Mr. N.T. Tharun
Director

Textile Technology professional and entrepreneur, with a B.Tech in Textile Technology (2020) and over five years of experience across apparel manufacturing, textile operations, and business management. He went on to complete the Global Family Managed Business programme at SP Jain School of Global Management (2023–2024), combining classroom learning with practical industry and family-business exposure. In August 2025, he joined Thiru Synergy as Director, bringing his technical, operational, and management background to the company's growth in renewable energy.
